# Farmio — Game Vision

## Elevator Pitch

A top-down farming automation game where you manage a growing farm operation. You don't do the work — your workers do. Place buildings, set priorities, optimize transport routes, and build an ever-expanding automated mega-farm.

## Core Fantasy

You're not a farmer — you're a farm manager building an empire. Start with a few family workers and a small plot, and grow into a sprawling operation with dozens of workers, vehicles, and production chains.

## Inspirations

- **Factorio**: Grid-based placement, logistics puzzles, automation scaling, top-down perspective
- **Farming Simulator**: Crop cycles, vehicles, farming theming, progression through equipment
- **Rimworld/Oxygen Not Included**: Task queue, worker priorities, automation

## Key Pillars

1. **Automation over action** — The player plans and manages, never does manual labor
2. **Visible workforce** — Watching your workers and vehicles bustle around is the payoff
3. **Simple to start, deep to optimize** — Early game is drag-and-drop easy, late game rewards careful logistics planning
4. **Peaceful & satisfying** — No combat, no threats (for now). The joy is in building and growing.

## Technical Decisions

- **Engine**: Godot 4.x
- **Language**: GDScript
- **Rendering**: Low-poly 3D with fixed orthographic camera (top-down, no rotation)
- **Placement**: Grid-based (1 tile = 3m x 3m)
- **Target platform**: PC (initially)

## Endgame Goal

Fully automated mega-farm — a massive, self-sustaining operation where every crop is planted, harvested, processed, and sold without player intervention.
