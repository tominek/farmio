# Future Ideas & Parking Lot

Ideas that are interesting but not core to the initial version. Parked here so we don't forget them but don't let them creep into scope.

## Economy
- Dynamic market prices (supply/demand — selling too much of one thing drops its price)
- Land purchase to expand available grid
- Achievements / milestones as secondary goals
- Loans / debt system

## Challenges & Threats (post-peaceful phase)
- Pest infestations damaging crops
- Weather events (drought, storms)
- Market price fluctuations
- Equipment breakdowns requiring repair

## Expanded Systems
- Seasons (spring/summer/autumn/winter) — affects crop growth, workers shift to processing/wood in winter
- Crop rotation and soil health
- Spoilage as a difficulty setting (goods expire if not processed/sold)
- Greenhouses: controlled environment, requires water infrastructure, higher yield, year-round growing, more labor-intensive
- Fertilizer / spray production chains (in-house instead of buying)
- Animal goods: Dairy Barn → milk → cheese, Chicken Coop → eggs
- Animal breeding
- Additional field crops: Sunflowers → oil, Rapeseed/Canola → oil, Barley → malt → beer
- Greenhouse crops: Tomatoes → sauce, Peppers, Herbs, etc.
- Tree farms / orchards: Apples, Pears, Cherries, etc.
- Baler (tractor attachment) — bales straw/hay after harvest, needed for animal feed
- Mower (tractor attachment) — cuts grass/hay for animal feed
- Soil fertility / terrain variation affecting field placement
- Bushes, stones as additional clearable natural obstacles

## Social / World
- Neighboring farms / NPC traders
- Town with shops, upgrades, contracts
- Contract system: "Deliver 50 bread by Tuesday for bonus payment"
- Reputation system affecting prices

## Commerce
- Market Stall — on-farm selling at lower prices (convenience vs. Dealer's better prices, trade-off mechanic)
- Dealer delivery service — pay extra fee for the Dealer to deliver supplies to a player-placed drop-off point on the farm, workers collect from there (convenience vs. cost trade-off)

## Transport
- Multi-stop pickup routes (pick up from multiple buildings in one trip)
- Traffic signs (yield, stop) for intersection priority control
- Traffic lights for high-traffic intersections
- Traffic density / most-used paths overlay
- Front Loader (utility vehicle for loading/unloading at storage and processing buildings)

## Worker Management
- Click to select individual worker, see their info/task
- Drag-select or shift-click to select groups of workers
- Right-click to send selected workers to a location
- Worker restrictions: zone, task type, building lock

## Advanced Automation
- Programmable worker schedules
- Conditional logic: "If storage > 80%, switch to selling"
- Drone delivery (futuristic tier)
- Automated greenhouses

## Polish & Feel
- Seasonal visual changes (snow, autumn leaves)
- Worker idle animations, personality
- Farm animals wandering
- Sound design: ambient farm sounds, machinery
- Photo mode for sharing farm layouts

## Multiplayer
- Co-op farm management
- Competing farms with shared market
- Trading between players
