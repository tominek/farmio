# Economy & Progression

## Currency

- **Money** — earned by selling goods at the Dealer
- Used to: hire workers, buy buildings, purchase vehicles/equipment, buy seeds/fertilizer/spray, unlock research

## Progression Loop

```
Place buildings → Workers produce goods → Sell at Dealer → Earn money
	↑                                                         ↓
	←──── Buy buildings / workers / vehicles / supplies ←─────
```

Planks act as a secondary resource gate for mid/late-game buildings (Tree Farm → Sawmill → planks).

## Starting Conditions

The player begins with:
- **Dealer** placed somewhere on the map
- **Pickup point** at the farm origin — starting Garage + Storage Barn
- **Dirt road** connecting the two, winding around dense vegetation (not a straight line)
- 2-3 workers (family members, free)
- 1 Light Pickup Truck (in the starting Garage)
- Enough money to place a small crop field and buy first seeds

The pickup point is the farm's hub — player expands outward from there in any direction. The winding dirt road to the Dealer can be upgraded or bypassed with a straighter shortcut through cleared land later.

## Early Game (Minutes 0-30)

**Player is learning the basics:**
- Place a small crop field, drive to Dealer to buy potato seeds
- Workers plant and harvest by hand
- Sell raw potatoes at the Dealer for small income
- Hire a few more workers, place more fields
- Plant a Tree Farm early (trees grow slow — plan ahead for planks)

**Available:** Small crop fields, Storage Barn, Garage, Dirt/Gravel roads, Hand Mill, Sawmill
**Goal:** Establish basic income, learn the crop cycle

## Mid Game (Hours 1-3)

**Player is building production chains:**
- First trees are ready — Sawmill produces planks
- Build processing buildings (Water Mill, Bakery) using money + planks
- Set up the bread chain: Wheat → Mill → Flour → Bakery → Bread
- Buy first tractor + basic attachments for medium fields
- Upgrade to Heavy Pickup + trailer for bigger Dealer hauls
- Build Supply Storage near fields for seeds/fertilizer
- Start laying cobblestone/asphalt on busy routes
- Configure auto-sell thresholds at the Dealer

**Available:** Medium fields, all processing buildings, basic tractors, trailers, better roads
**Goal:** First complete production chain running, Dealer trips efficient

## Late Game (Hours 3+)

**Player is optimizing the mega-farm:**
- Large and Mega fields with advanced tractors + combines
- Multiple parallel production chains (bread, pasta, sugar, planks)
- Optimized road network, one-way loops, loading bay upgrades
- Auto-sell keeps money flowing without manual intervention
- 20+ workers, equipment contention drives more vehicle purchases
- The farm runs itself

**Available:** All field tiers, advanced tractors/combines, all road types, all upgrades
**Goal:** Fully automated mega-farm

## Research / Tech Tree

Unlocking new buildings, field tiers, vehicles, and upgrades requires research.

- **Cost:** Money (single currency keeps it simple)
- **Structure:** Branching tree — player chooses what to prioritize

### Branches (draft)

**Fields & Crops:**
- Medium Fields → Large Fields → Mega Fields
- New crop types (sugar beet, etc.)
- Better seed varieties (higher yield)

**Processing:**
- Hand Mill → Water Mill → Industrial Mill
- Bakery → Pasta Maker
- Sugar Mill

**Vehicles & Equipment:**
- Basic Tractor → Advanced Tractor
- Basic Combine → Large Combine
- Heavy Pickup → Trailers (Small → Medium → Large)
- Tractor attachments (seeders, sprayers, harvesters, grain cart)

**Infrastructure:**
- Road tiers (Cobblestone → Asphalt → Concrete)
- Building upgrades (loading bays, capacity upgrades)
- Silo, Supply Storage

**Forestry:**
- Sawmill
- Larger tree farms

The branching means a player could rush tractors for bigger fields, or rush processing for higher-value goods. Different strategies, both viable.

## Income Balance

- **Raw crops:** Low margin, fast cycle, no infrastructure needed
- **Processed goods:** High margin, slow cycle, needs buildings + planks
- **Planks:** Can be sold for profit OR used for construction — player decides
- **Dealer trips:** Transport time is a real cost — bigger loads per trip = more efficient

The player constantly balances:
- Selling raw vs. investing in processing
- Using planks for building vs. selling them
- Buying more workers vs. more vehicles vs. more buildings
- Transport capacity vs. production capacity

## Bankruptcy Protection

The player should **never be stuck** with no way to earn money. Safety nets:

- **Natural trees** spawn on the map. Workers can always chop them down and sell the wood at the Dealer — no buildings, no seeds, no investment needed. This is the fallback income.
- Crop fields can be demolished for full refund
- Workers have no salary — they don't drain money by existing
- The Dealer always buys everything (no demand limits)

Even if a player makes bad decisions and runs out of money, they can always send a worker to chop trees and earn enough to recover.
